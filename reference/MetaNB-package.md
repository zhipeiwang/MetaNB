# MetaNB: Meta-Analysis of Net Benefit and Value-of-Information

Performs Bayesian trivariate random-effects meta-analysis of prevalence,
sensitivity, and specificity to estimate net benefit across
heterogeneous validation settings (Wynants et al., 2018)
[doi:10.1002/sim.7653](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.7653) . Using Markov
chain Monte Carlo sampling via JAGS, it produces estimates of net
benefit and its credible and prediction intervals, and the posterior
probability a model is clinically useful in a new setting, for given
relevant harm-to-benefit ratios. It further estimates
value-of-information metrics, including expected value of perfect and
partial perfect information, to quantify uncertainty for the decision to
implement the model in care across heterogeneous settings.
